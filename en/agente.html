How it works

1. PlanRewrites the question in legal wording.
2. SearchHybrid: keywords and vectors, within the sector.
3. Read in isolationOne fragment per call. Nothing piles up.
4. Take notesOnly a verbatim quote survives, checked by code.
5. AnswerFrom notes only, with article and page.
