Conclusion

  • Held-out: agent 4/8, simple RAG 4/8, stuffing 6/8. The agent does not beat the baselines: higher accuracy cannot be claimed.
  • Development: agent 12/15, simple RAG 10/15, stuffing 10/15. These are the questions the agent was tuned on.
  • Held-out bottleneck: retrieval (3 of 4 failures).
  • What does hold: a 525-token peak window versus 3703 for stuffing and 1455 for simple RAG.
  • The price: 3.3x the tokens and 4.7x the time (p50) of simple RAG.
  • Agent confident errors on held-out: 3. A verbatim quote does not guarantee it comes from the right article.

Manual review: It gives the deadline to approve the certificate (30 days from delivery), not the payment deadline (30 days from approval). The number matches by chance and the pattern accepted it.
